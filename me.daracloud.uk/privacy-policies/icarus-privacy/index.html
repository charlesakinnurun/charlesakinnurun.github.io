Privacy Policy

2 min · Dara Adedeji

Effective Date: 4/4/25

Thank you for using Icarus! This privacy policy informs you about how we handle data within the Icarus application.

  1. No Data Collection

Icarus operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data or information created within the application. All data, including whiteboard strategies and any other content you create, remains solely on your device.

  1. No Internet Connectivity

Icarus does not connect to the internet. Therefore, there is no opportunity for data to be transmitted from your device to us or any third party.

  1. Open Source Nature

Icarus is a Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) application. The source code is publicly available, allowing you to verify the application’s behavior and confirm that no data collection occurs.

  1. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As Icarus currently does not collect any data, significant changes to this privacy policy are unlikely. However, if future versions of the application introduce features that involve data handling, we will update this policy accordingly and provide clear notice to users.

  1. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us through the project’s open-source repository or other communication channels provided there. Via the issue tracker on the project’s code repository.

This privacy policy is effective as of the date stated above. We are committed to maintaining your privacy and ensuring transparency in our application’s operation.
